# Open-Source Assets & Attribution

Highway Surfers: Most Wanted (WebGL 3D) uses verified open-source 3D models and real recorded audio samples:

## 3D Vehicle Models (`.glb`)
- **`ferrari.glb`** — Ferrari 458 Italia from [mrdoob/three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js) (`examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb`), **MIT License**.
- **`ocean_parked.glb` & `ocean_convertible.glb`** — 8 Blender-modeled modern & classic vehicles (`coupe`, `sedan`, `hatch`, `suv`, `pickup`, `wagon`, `crossover`, `convertible`) from [StarKnightt/ocean-drive](https://github.com/StarKnightt/ocean-drive), **MIT License**.
- **`race_proto.glb`** — GT race prototype (`car.glb`) from [DanieloM83/R3F-Car-Racing](https://github.com/DanieloM83/R3F-Car-Racing).
- **Draco & Meshopt Decoders** — Bundled locally from Three.js (`three/examples/jsm/libs/draco/gltf/` & `meshopt_decoder.module.js`), **Apache-2.0 / MIT License**.

## Recorded Audio Samples (`.mp3`, transcoded mono 44.1 kHz for iOS Safari)
- **`engine_sport.mp3`, `engine_rev.mp3`, `nitro_boost.mp3`, `tire_screech.mp3`, `crash.mp3`, `horn.mp3`** — from [pmndrs/racing-game](https://github.com/pmndrs/racing-game), **MIT License**.
- **`engine_v8.mp3`, `wind_rush.mp3`** — from [mnursey/Real-Time-Car-Audio-Emitter](https://github.com/mnursey/Real-Time-Car-Audio-Emitter).
- **`jet_afterburner.mp3`, `jet_turbine.mp3`, `jet_ignite.mp3`** — Real F-16 Fighting Falcon turbine & afterburner recordings from [NikolaiVChr/f16](https://github.com/NikolaiVChr/f16) (FlightGear), **GPL-2.0 License**.
- **`police_siren.mp3`, `coin.mp3`** — from [jonjonsson/SoundMonster](https://github.com/jonjonsson/SoundMonster), **CC0 1.0 Public Domain**.
